## Flow stages

1. A customer submits a review through [[service|Review API]] using [[event|reviews/submit-review]].
2. [[service|Review API]] stores the review in **Review Database** and publishes [[event|reviews-platform/reviews/review-submitted]].
3. [[service|Review Moderation Worker]] evaluates the review.
4. The worker publishes [[event|reviews-platform/reviews/review-published]] or [[event|reviews-platform/reviews/review-rejected]].
5. [[service|Rating Aggregator]] consumes published reviews and updates **Rating Cache**.
6. [[service|Review API]] serves [[event|reviews/get-product-reviews]] for storefront reads.

## Moderation boundary

Moderation is asynchronous so submission remains responsive and moderation rules can evolve independently.

## Rating boundary

Ratings are a read model. **Rating Cache** is optimised for product pages, while **Review Database** remains the durable review store.

## Follow-up interactions

After publication, customers can use [[event|reviews/vote-review-helpful]] or [[event|reviews/flag-review]]. These interactions publish [[event|reviews-platform/reviews/review-helpful-voted]] and [[event|reviews-platform/reviews/review-flagged]].
