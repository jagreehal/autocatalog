[[domain|fulfilment]] gets confirmed orders to customers. It owns the operational handoff from stock reservation through warehouse work and carrier delivery.

## System context map

<Map />

## Inventory System

[[system|inventory-system]] owns **Inventory Database**. [[service|Inventory Service]] handles [[event|ordering/reserve-inventory]], [[event|fulfilment/release-inventory]] and [[event|fulfilment/get-stock-level]].

It publishes [[event|fulfilment-platform/fulfilment/inventory-reserved]] or [[event|fulfilment-platform/fulfilment/inventory-unavailable]] during checkout.

## Warehouse System

[[system|warehouse-system]] owns picking and packing in **Warehouse Database**. [[service|Warehouse Service]] reacts to [[event|ordering-platform/ordering/order-completed]] and publishes [[event|fulfilment-platform/fulfilment/order-ready-for-shipping]].

[[service|Picking Worker]] handles packing work and publishes [[event|fulfilment-platform/fulfilment/order-packed]].

## Shipping System and Carrier

[[system|shipping-system]] creates shipments through [[event|fulfilment/create-shipment]]. [[system|carrier]] is the external delivery provider and reports [[event|fulfilment-platform/fulfilment/shipment-created]], [[event|fulfilment-platform/fulfilment/shipment-delivered]] or [[event|fulfilment-platform/fulfilment/shipment-failed]].

## Boundary with Ordering

Ordering decides that an order exists. Fulfilment decides whether and how it can be delivered. Keep these lifecycles separate so cancellation, refunds and shipment exceptions can be handled independently.
