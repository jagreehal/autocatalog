[[domain|payments]] owns payment intent and payment outcomes. It integrates with external providers but keeps Acme's internal payment state in **Payment Database**.

## System context map

<Map />

## Payment Processing System

[[system|payment-processing-system]] is composed of [[service|Payment API]] and [[service|Payment Worker]].

[[service|Payment API]] receives [[event|ordering/authorize-payment]] from checkout and records the intent. [[service|Payment Worker]] drives the external charge/refund process and records outcomes.

Important events:

- [[event|payments-platform/payments/payment-requested]]
- [[event|payments-platform/payments/payment-succeeded]]
- [[event|payments-platform/payments/payment-failed]]
- [[event|payments-platform/payments/refund-requested]]

## External providers

[[system|stripe]] is the external payment processor. It reports outcomes through [[service|Webhook Endpoint]] events such as [[event|payments-platform/payments/payment-succeeded]], [[event|payments-platform/payments/payment-failed]] and [[event|payments-platform/payments/refund-processed]].

[[system|fraud-detection]] screens payment attempts and reports [[event|payments-platform/payments/fraud-check-passed]] or [[event|payments-platform/payments/fraud-check-failed]].

## Failure handling

Payments cross external boundaries, so every step should be durable and retryable. Do not assume webhook delivery order. Payment state transitions should be idempotent and traceable through the payment database.
