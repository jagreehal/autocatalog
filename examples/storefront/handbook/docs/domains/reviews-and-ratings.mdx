[[domain|reviews]] owns customer feedback on products. Unlike the other domains, it is modelled directly with services and data stores rather than nested systems.

## Domain map

<Map />

## Services

[[service|Review API]] accepts review commands and serves product reviews. It writes to **Review Database** and reads aggregate data from **Rating Cache**.

[[service|Review Moderation Worker]] consumes [[event|reviews-platform/reviews/review-submitted]] and publishes either [[event|reviews-platform/reviews/review-published]] or [[event|reviews-platform/reviews/review-rejected]].

[[service|Rating Aggregator]] reacts to published reviews and updates the product rating model, publishing [[event|reviews-platform/reviews/rating-updated]].

## Core entity

**Review** is the central aggregate. It moves from submitted to moderated to published or rejected.

## Product impact

Reviews influence product discovery and conversion, but they do not own product data. Product identity remains in [[domain|catalog]], while review state remains in Reviews.
