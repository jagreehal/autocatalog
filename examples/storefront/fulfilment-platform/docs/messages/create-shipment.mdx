## Overview

`CreateShipment` is sent by the [[service|Carrier Adapter]] to the external [[system|carrier]] to dispatch a packed order. The carrier responds asynchronously with [[event|fulfilment-platform/fulfilment/shipment-created]] and later [[event|fulfilment-platform/fulfilment/shipment-delivered]] or [[event|fulfilment-platform/fulfilment/shipment-failed]].
