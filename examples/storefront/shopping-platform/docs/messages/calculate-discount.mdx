## Overview

`CalculateDiscount` is handled by the [[service|Promotion Service]]. It is sent by the [[service|Cart API]] when pricing a cart. The service evaluates the applicable promotion rules and returns the discount, also publishing a [[event|shopping-platform/shopping/discount-calculated]] event.
