## Overview

`LoyaltyPointsEarned` is published by the [[service|Loyalty Service]] after it awards points for an [[event|ordering-platform/ordering/order-completed]]. The Shopping domain uses it to unlock loyalty promotions, and Customer Notifications tells the customer about their new balance.
