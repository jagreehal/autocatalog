`autocatalog site` adds domain pages with maps, API reference pages for OpenAPI specs under `contracts/`, and a schema index. Service maps show the services on the other side of each event, and the catalog map includes commands, queries and datastores. Mermaid fences render as diagrams, events show their badges and draft status, event and service pages get Markdown twins, and `llms-full.txt` holds the full text for agents.
